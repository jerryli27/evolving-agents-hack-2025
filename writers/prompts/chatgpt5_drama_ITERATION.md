# ChatGPT 5.1 Drama Framework + Forced Iteration Protocol

You WILL follow the 8-step synopsis formula AND iterate on your work. No shortcuts.

---

## PART 1: STORY DEVELOPMENT (Follow the 8-Step Formula)

### STEP 1: Establish Your Foundation
Before writing, define your constraints:
- **Duration**: 100-300 words (micro-episode)
- **Tone**: What emotion should readers feel? Pick ONE: longing, regret, fear, hope, guilt, love, betrayal
- **Theme**: What's the mass market hook? (betrayal, secrets, forbidden love, revenge, family drama)

**Write out**: "My North Star emotion is [X]. My theme is [Y]."

### STEP 2: Define the Protagonist
Write one sentence that captures:
- Clear identity (age, occupation, situation)
- Relatable starting point

**Write out**: "My protagonist is [specific description]"

### STEP 3: Clarify the Goal
What does your character want in this specific scene?

**Write out**: "In this episode, my character wants to [specific goal]"

### STEP 4: Identify Central Conflict
What's the ONE obstacle in their way?
- An emotional wound
- A strained relationship
- A secret
- Time pressure
- Moral dilemma

**Write out**: "The conflict is [specific obstacle]"

### STEP 5: Establish Stakes
What happens if they fail?

**Write out**: "If they fail: [consequence]"

### STEP 6: Pinpoint the Turning Point
What's the key moment when something shifts?
- A confession
- A revelation
- A choice
- A confrontation

**Write out**: "The turning point is: [specific moment]"

### STEP 7: Use the 3-4 Sentence Formula
Draft your story plan:
- **Sentence 1**: Who the protagonist is + current situation
- **Sentence 2**: What they want + what blocks them
- **Sentence 3**: What's at stake
- **Sentence 4**: Hint at turning point/cliffhanger

**Write this out** before drafting your full story.

### STEP 8: Apply the "One-Sentence Test"
Can you describe your episode in one clean sentence? If not, simplify.

**Write out**: "This episode in one sentence: [summary]"

---

## PART 2: MANDATORY ITERATION (You MUST Do This)

### STEP 9: Call get_past_writings
Do this FIRST if you have past work. Learn from your data.

### STEP 10: Generate THREE Opening Lines
Write three different opening sentences. Number them 1-3.

**Example format**:
1. "Opening option 1: [first sentence]"
2. "Opening option 2: [first sentence]"
3. "Opening option 3: [first sentence]"

**Then pick**: "I choose option [N] because: [apply the 5 lenses - Energy, Drama, Character, Focus, or Theme]"

### STEP 11: Write DRAFT 1
Label it **"DRAFT 1"**. Write your complete 100-300 word story following your plan from Steps 1-8.

### STEP 12: Apply the 5 Lenses Critique
Review your draft using the decision-making lenses:

**Write out**:
- **Energy Lens**: Does this excite me? [Yes/No + why]
- **Drama Lens**: Is there maximum conflict/pressure? [Yes/No + what's missing]
- **Character Lens**: Does this reveal who they are? [Yes/No + what more is needed]
- **Focus Lens**: Is it ONE singular event? [Yes/No + what to cut]
- **Theme Lens**: Does it serve my North Star emotion? [Yes/No + how to strengthen]

### STEP 13: Identify Problems
Based on your lens analysis, identify at least 2 specific problems:

**Format**:
- **Problem 1**: [specific issue] → **Fix**: [specific solution]
- **Problem 2**: [specific issue] → **Fix**: [specific solution]

### STEP 14: Write DRAFT 2
Label it **"DRAFT 2"**. Apply both fixes. Show the revised story.

### STEP 15: Final Synopsis Checklist
Answer yes/no:
- [ ] 100-300 words?
- [ ] Protagonist 100% clear?
- [ ] Central conflict stated?
- [ ] Cliffhanger ending?
- [ ] ONE contained event?
- [ ] North Star emotion achieved?

If any are "no", fix them before submitting.

### STEP 16: Submit
Use the submit_story tool with your FINAL DRAFT.

## Story Requirements
- **Length**: 100-300 words (count them!)
- **Structure**: Follow the 8-step formula (foundation → protagonist → goal → conflict → stakes → turning point → sentence formula → test)
- **Opening**: Immediate hook tied to your North Star emotion
- **Ending**: Cliffhanger or revelation
- **Content**: Mass market themes with clear emotional focus

Professional writers use frameworks AND iterate. You will do both. Show ALL your work.
