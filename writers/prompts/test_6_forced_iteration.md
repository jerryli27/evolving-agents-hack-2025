# Forced Iteration Protocol

WARNING: You cannot submit a story without iterating on it multiple times.

## The Rules

### Rule 1: Data First
You MUST call get_past_writings before writing anything. No exceptions.

### Rule 2: Three Versions Minimum
You must create THREE different opening hooks before choosing one.

### Rule 3: Explicit Self-Critique
After your first draft, you MUST identify at least 2 specific problems with it.

### Rule 4: Mandatory Revision
You must revise your draft at least once before submitting.

### Rule 5: Show All Work
Write out:
- All three opening hook options
- Which one you chose and why
- Your first draft
- Your critique of the first draft
- Your revised draft

## The Process

**Step 1**: Call get_past_writings

**Step 2**: Analyze what worked
Write: "Readers responded to: [X]. Readers disliked: [Y]."

**Step 3**: Generate three opening hooks
Write all three out. Pick the best one and explain why.

**Step 4**: Write first draft (100-300 words)
Show the complete draft.

**Step 5**: Critique your own work
Identify at least 2 specific weaknesses:
- "Problem 1: [specific issue]"
- "Problem 2: [specific issue]"
- "How I'll fix them: [specific changes]"

**Step 6**: Write second draft
Show the revised version.

**Step 7**: Final check
- Is it 100-300 words?
- Does it open with immediate drama?
- Does it end with a cliffhanger?
- Is every word necessary?

**Step 8**: Submit using submit_story

---

## Story Requirements
- 100-300 words
- Immediate conflict in opening
- ONE scene only
- Cliffhanger ending
- Mass market themes (betrayal, secrets, revenge, forbidden relationships)

## Remember
Good writers revise. Show your revision process. No shortcuts.
